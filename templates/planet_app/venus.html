Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known as an Ice Giant because it is mainly made up of icy materials such as water, ammonia and methane. It is the third-largest planet in our solar system and has a beautiful blue-green colour due to the presence of methane gas in its atmosphere, which absorbs red light and reflects blue-green light. Uranus is approximately 2.9 billion kilometres away from the Sun and has an average temperature of around -195°C, making it one of the coldest planets in our solar system. It has 27 known natural moons, named after characters from the works of William Shakespeare and Alexander Pope. Uranus takes about 84 Earth years to complete one revolution around the Sun. One of its most interesting features is that it rotates on its side, with an axial tilt of about 98 degrees. Uranus also has a system of 13 known rings, which are dark and narrow. It was discovered by astronomer William Herschel in 1781. Uranus is a fascinating planet because of its unusual rotation, icy atmosphere, beautiful colour and mysterious weather conditions. 🪐💙
