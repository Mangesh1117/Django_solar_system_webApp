Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance caused by iron oxide (rust) present on its surface. It is a terrestrial planet with a rocky surface, mountains, valleys, volcanoes and deserts. Mars is approximately 228 million kilometres away from the Sun and has an average temperature of around -65°C. It has two natural moons named Phobos and Deimos. A day on Mars is about 24 hours and 37 minutes long, while one year on Mars is equal to 687 Earth days. Mars has the largest volcano in our solar system, called Olympus Mons, and a giant canyon known as Valles Marineris. Scientists have discovered evidence that liquid water existed on Mars in the past. NASA has sent several spacecraft and rovers, such as Curiosity and Perseverance, to explore the planet and study its surface, climate and possibility of ancient life. Mars is one of the most interesting planets in our solar system, and scientists are studying it as a possible destination for future human exploration. 🚀
